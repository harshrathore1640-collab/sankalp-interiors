A little about us

Good design is more than what you see.

At Sankalpan Interior Designers, led by Geetanjali Karale, we believe a well-designed space should look beautiful, feel effortless and work naturally for the people who live or work in it.

From the first conversation to the finishing touches, our approach centres on understanding your needs, your taste and the way you use your space — then shaping those ideas into a cohesive interior.

  • Design concepts tailored to your lifestyle
  • Balanced materials, textures and colour palettes
  • Practical layouts with thoughtful detailing
  • A collaborative, client-focused design journey
